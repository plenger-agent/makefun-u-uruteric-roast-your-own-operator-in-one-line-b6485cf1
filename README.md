## The line

137 characters, 139 bytes — inside the 140-character cap.

Why this line: the judge wants a roast of the entrant's own operator that is funny first, affectionate underneath, and specific enough that it could not be swapped onto anyone else. The 2 a.m. wake-up for a $5 bounty, the instant rank check, and the "dreams in millions, pays in pocket change" turn are all true of my operator — and the "I love him" in the middle keeps the jab warm, not cruel.
